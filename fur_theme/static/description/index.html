Fur - Furniture eCommerce Theme

A complete, ready-to-sell storefront for furniture and interior design shops

Homepage, shop, category and product pages, a one-step checkout on the cart page, a projects showcase and an instant product search, in a bright yellow and navy design that works on every screen. Odoo 20.0, Community and Enterprise.

See it in action

What you get

  • Header: your logo, instant product search with a results dropdown, hotline, cart, account and flag language switcher.
  • Homepage: hero slider, service strip, category grid, one product block per category with sub-category pills, featured projects.
  • Mega menu: top categories in the menu bar, sub-categories dropping down on hover; off-canvas menu on phones.
  • Shop & category pages: banner with breadcrumb, sub-category pills, product cards with crossed-out prices, wishlist and quick add to cart.
  • Product page: code and category, perks, hotline card, sticky buy box, description and buying guide tabs, related products.
  • One-step checkout: name, phone, address and payment method on the cart page (bank transfer, cash on delivery), then Odoo's own payment and confirmation flow.
  • Projects: showcase your interior projects on /projects, with type filters, detail pages and the products used.
  • Floating contact buttons: call, WhatsApp, Zalo and Messenger.
  • Accent-insensitive search that ranks product codes and full-word matches first.
  • English and Vietnamese included; every text is translatable.
  • Theme can be switched off per website to get Odoo's own layout back.

Setup

  1. Install Fur - Furniture eCommerce Theme (dependencies: Website Sale, Custom payment provider).
  2. Website > Configuration > Websites > Fur Theme: brand name, footer logo, hotline, WhatsApp, Zalo, Messenger, opening hours, buying guide.
  3. Upload your logo in Website > Configuration > Settings.
  4. Tick Show on Homepage on eCommerce categories and Featured on Homepage on products and projects.
  5. Set your bank account on the Bank transfer payment provider.

With demo data, a complete sample furniture shop is created (categories, 34 products, 6 projects).
